TRADITIONAL RITUALS & SERVICES

Sacred Cambodian Ceremonies

❀
MORNING PROCESSION

ពិធីហែជំនូន (Hai Chenh Chnoon)

The groom's vibrant procession bearing trays of fruits, traditional gifts, and sacred offerings to the bride's family home.

CLEANSING RITUAL

ពិធីកាត់សក់ (Gaat Sah)

Symbolic hair-cutting and perfume blessing by elders to cleanse past burdens and welcome new marital happiness.

PAIRING & KNOT TYING

ពិធីសំពះផ្ទឹម & ចងដៃ (Phtim)

Tying red threads around the couple's wrists accompanied by sword blessings, flower showers, and traditional songs.
